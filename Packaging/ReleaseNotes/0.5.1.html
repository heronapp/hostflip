What's New

  • Windows open where you are. Opening hostflip or its Settings from the menu bar now brings the window to your current desktop (Space) and in front of other apps, instead of leaving it on the desktop where you last used it.
  • Clearer error messages. When the background helper cannot be reached, hostflip explains what happened and what to try next instead of showing an internal error name. Save, import, and export failures read as plain sentences too.
  • Small fixes. The drift review stays open while a reconciliation is running, the “Cannot Open Workspace” screen is fully translated, and Relaunch Now no longer quits the app if the relaunch cannot start.
