What's New

  • Search every profile. A search field at the top of the sidebar (Edit › Find in All Profiles…, ⌘⇧F) finds a hostname, IP, or any text across Base Hosts and all your profiles, grouped by profile with the active ones marked. Pick a result to jump straight to that line.
  • Toggle Comment. ⌘/ comments or uncomments the current line or every selected line, as one undoable step — no more typing # by hand to switch a mapping off.
  • Incomplete lines are flagged. A line with an IP but no hostname (or a lone word) gets a warning marker in the line-number gutter with an explanation on hover. Saving is never blocked; the marker just tells you the entry will not take effect.
  • Smooth with very large profiles. Remote Profiles with tens of thousands of lines now open instantly and scroll without stutter. Long lines wrap in the editor instead of scrolling sideways.
  • Report an issue from the app. Help › Report an Issue… opens a prefilled bug report, and Copy Diagnostic Report puts your versions, helper state, and profile counts on the clipboard — never your hosts content, profile names, or URLs.
